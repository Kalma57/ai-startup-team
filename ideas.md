# Startup ideas for runs and the live demo

Easy (lots of competitors, research works well):
- An app that matches students with part-time jobs near campus
- A tool that turns lecture recordings into flashcards and practice exams
- A marketplace for renting camping gear from neighbors

Medium:
- An AI assistant for small restaurants that predicts tomorrow's ingredient orders
- A platform for splitting apartment bills and chores between roommates in Israel
- אפליקציה שמחברת בין סטודנטים לטיפולי שיניים מוזלים בפקולטות לרפואת שיניים

Hard (good for showing QA and traces under pressure):
- Insurance for freelancers' laptops, priced per day of use
- A B2B tool that audits AI agents' tool calls for security risks
