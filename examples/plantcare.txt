PlantCare is an iPhone app, in Hebrew first, that tells you each morning which plants need water and what to do when one looks sick.
Adding a plant takes just a name or a photo. The AI recommends a care plan, and the user can accept it, set their own schedule, or set one rule for everything.
Photo ID and diagnosis use AI, while reminders and plant info work offline, so the app is simple enough for forgetful, low-tech users.
Running costs are low, about $99/year for Apple plus a small server and a few dollars a month in AI calls.
We start as a free private app for one user, learn from real use, then open it to more people with a freemium model.
